# PCB Business Card

Hi! I am **Vasil Terziev**, a student and hardware enthusiast.

This repository contains the design for my custom printed circuit board (PCB) business card. Instead of traditional paper, I designed a functional PCB to share my contact information and showcase my electronics skills.

---

## Technical Details

- **Microcontroller / IC:** TQFP-32 package footprint
- **Components:** SMD resistors and capacitors
- **Finish:** Blue solder mask with clear white silkscreen text
- **Layout:** Optimized trace routing and clean aesthetic

## How to Manufacture

1. Download the Gerber files or project files from this repository.
2. Order the PCB from your preferred manufacturer (e.g., JLCPCB, PCBWay).
3. Solder the SMD components using a soldering iron with a fine tip or hot air reflow.
