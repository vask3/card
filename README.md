# PCB Business Card

Hi! I am **Vasil Terziev**, a student and hardware enthusiast.

This repository contains the design and layout files for my custom printed circuit board (PCB) business card. Instead of handing out a standard paper card, I designed a physical, electronics-inspired card to share my contact details, demonstrate clean PCB design practices, and reflect my interest in hardware and circuit layout.

---

## Overview

The goal of this project is to combine practical contact information with thoughtful hardware aesthetics. Every trace, pad, and silkscreen label is arranged so the board looks intentional and clean while remaining easy to fabricate. The component footprints on the board are purely decorative—no soldering or component assembly is required, making the bare PCB itself the finished business card.

---

## Technical Details

- **Microcontroller / IC Footprint:** Decorative TQFP-32 package footprint included for visual styling
- **Passive Footprints:** Decorative SMD resistor and capacitor pads integrated into the layout design
- **Solder Mask & Silkscreen:** Blue solder mask paired with crisp, high-contrast white silkscreen text and markings
- **PCB Layout:** Carefully routed traces with clean geometry, consistent spacing, and a balanced front/back visual flow
- **Assembly:** None required—designed to be used as a bare, finished PCB right out of fabrication

## How to Manufacture

1. **Prepare Fabrication Files:** Download the Gerber files (or open the source project files and export Gerbers/drill files) from this repository.
2. **Order the PCB:** Upload the Gerber archive to your preferred PCB manufacturer (such as JLCPCB, PCBWay, or another fab house) and select a blue solder mask with white silkscreen.
3. **Ready to Use:** Since all component footprints are purely decorative, no soldering or parts sourcing is needed—your cards are ready to hand out as soon as they arrive.
